How to join

  1. Choose your available number/s from 1 to 100.

  5. Your slot is officially secured only after payment has been received and verified by Got Cha Dink Club.

Prize structure

  • 🥇 1st Prize — Kamito Alpha X Her Purple Paddle

Winner rule

Only one prize may be won by each person per draw.

If a player owns multiple slot numbers and one of their numbers is drawn as a winner, all of that player’s remaining numbers will no longer be eligible for the succeeding prizes in that same draw.

If another number belonging to the same winner is drawn, a redraw will be conducted until a different winner is selected.

This ensures three different winners for every raffle draw.

Live draw

The draw will take place only after all 100 slots are fully paid and secured.

Once the board is complete, Got Cha Dink Club will announce the official Live Draw schedule.

The draw will be streamed live on the official Got Cha Dink Club Facebook page.

Additional rules

  1. All secured slots are final and non-refundable.
  2. All prizes are non-convertible to cash.
  3. Prizes may not be exchanged for another prize or cash equivalent.

Got Cha Dink Club reserves the right to verify payments and participant details before confirming a slot.
